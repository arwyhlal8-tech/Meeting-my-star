## 📖 About The Game

**Meeting My Star** puts you in the shoes of a passionate fan who has just won a special ticket to meet their ultimate favorite idol, **Star**! 

You have a limited amount of time to talk with her, express your love, ask her personal questions, or give her a gift. Make your choices carefully before security calls "Time is up!"

## ✨ Key Features

* 🎭 **Interactive Branching Choices:** Choose what to talk about and see Star's different reactions and expressions.
* 🎨 **Original Art:** Custom-drawn characters, expressions, and backgrounds.
* ✍️ **Custom Name System:** Name your main character whatever you like (with fun built-in rules and easter eggs!).
* ⏱️ **Multiple Outcomes:** Discover different responses based on how you interact with Star.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Download the latest game release.
2. Launch `Meeting My Star.exe`.
3. Enter your name and enjoy your special fan-meeting moment with Star!
